# Client billing rules

Issued demonstration invoices are rebuilt from stored shifts until they are posted. Each line is employment cost plus a management fee. The fee rate lives on the service contract. Ashanti's sample rate is 12 percent and Tema's sample rate is 10 percent.

Posting writes a balanced journal: accounts receivable is debited, and wages payable and agency fee revenue are credited. Posting the same week again reverses the earlier journal and writes a new one. The original lines stay in place.

A client receipt debits cash and credits accounts receivable, and it cannot exceed the outstanding invoice. Receipts are book entries, not a bank settlement. No VAT or other tax is added. PAYE and pension are still not calculated.
