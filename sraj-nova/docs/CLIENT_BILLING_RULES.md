# Client billing rules

Issued demonstration invoices are rebuilt from stored shifts. Each line is employment cost plus a management fee. The fee rate lives on the service contract. Ashanti's sample rate is 12 percent and Tema's sample rate is 10 percent.

No VAT, levy, or other tax is added. Invoice totals are not a statutory tax calculation. Receipts and payment settlement are not implemented, so an issued invoice remains outstanding.
