# System requirements

## Implemented in milestone 1

- One agency record for SRAJ NOVA, currency GHS, timezone Africa/Accra, country GH.
- Client organizations, sites, departments, positions, and service-contract dates.
- Employees, current deployments, and an explicit legal employer of either the agency or a named client.
- Today's shift assignments, including an unfilled shift.
- Attendance events that are not overwritten by a correction.
- Command-center counts limited to the caller's client scope.
- Sign-in with HttpOnly cookies. Production settings require authenticator enrollment for the agency owner before a session is issued.

## Not implemented

- Weekly payroll, PAYE, pension, payslips, and payment settlement.
- Client invoices, receipts, and the general ledger.
- Public job portal, interviews, offers, and onboarding checklists.
- Biometric device adapters and offline attendance sync.
- Employee and client portals beyond the current scoped pages.
- Native mobile applications.
