# Implementation roadmap

## Milestone 1 — implemented

Agency, access control, foundational records, and the command center. Operational numbers come from the database. Financial engines stay inactive. Backend tests passed, and the command center was checked in the browser against the API.

## Milestone 2 — next

Client contacts, rate cards, contract documents, markup, invoice terms, and staffing-request detail. Still no invoice totals until the billing milestone.

## Milestone 3

Recruitment, candidate records, onboarding tasks, and fuller employee history.

## Milestone 4

Shift planning, attendance adapters, overtime, leave, and timesheet approval.

## Milestone 5

Weekly payroll with effective-dated Ghana rules, after professional validation. No live tax claim before that review.

## Milestone 6

Payment adapters that do not treat a request as settlement, cost-plus billing, receivables, and the ledger.

## Milestone 7

Employee portal, client portal, and operational reports.

## Milestone 8

Permission-scoped analytics, security hardening, broader automated tests, and deployment. AI output must cite stored records and must not authorize hiring, payroll, or payments.
