# Project overview

SRAJ NOVA is a workforce outsourcing agency. It recruits and manages workers who are deployed to external client organizations such as factories, warehouses, and logistics sites. The agency is not the factory.

Milestone 1 is implemented: Django API, Next.js command center, authentication, role scope, agency configuration, and operational counts from the database. Sample companies and workers are synthetic.

Payroll, billing, payments, recruitment processing, and AI answers are not implemented. The command center does not invent those amounts.
